Independent source-based analyst review covered all thirty-six answers and their
structured parts: eighty-nine reviewed narrative/part units, zero identified
unsupported units and two uncertain units in the source-only arm. These units are
not necessarily independent atomic assertions. One cross-product diagnostic lacked
established applicability and was held uncertain. This is analyst judgment,
not machine-proved entailment, human adjudication or a claim of zero hallucinations.
Citation-field copying and literal product/version fields explain much of the
complete-response failure; the original thresholds were not relaxed.

All three partitions failed their citation, applicability and ambiguity gates;
validation and sealed also failed the evidence floor. Compiled knowledge reduced
complete evidence in this pilot. Lower tool counts or latency cannot compensate
for failed evidence and safety gates. No retrieval improvement is accepted.

Including construction, forty-four completed client model turns consumed
2,853,726 observed tokens and 2,042.05 measured model seconds. Each retrieval arm
reported fifty-six usage-update events; these are not independently counted backend
requests or hidden retries. A separate probe consumed 10,358 tokens. Monetary cost
was not reported and remains unknown. This small fixed-order study has one model
episode per question/arm, no confidence interval and no production-network claim.

A separate no-model text-query probe repeated eighteen identical questions three
times per arm: fifty-four authenticated local MCP queries each, reranking off,
unchanged sources/pages. Source-only P50/P95 were 56.05/106.18 milliseconds;
compiled P50/P95 were 58.31/104.98 milliseconds. These local ASGI/PostgreSQL samples
are neither a population service objective nor additional held-out quality evidence.
