Use C0 with ChatGPT

Search what you know, cite your sources, and save useful knowledge in your own C0 memory.

Checking your sign-in…

Sign in to C0

1 · Connect

Add C0 to ChatGPT

In ChatGPT web, open Plugins → personal → + → Add custom MCP server.

Use these details for this instance:

Choose OAuth and CIMD / Client ID Metadata Document. Leave the client ID and secret empty, then create the connection.

When C0 opens, sign in with your own account. If this is the first connection, follow the approval step, then choose the permissions to allow.

Checking connection details…

Need an administrator?

An administrator reviews a new ChatGPT connection before it can be used. Sign in to C0 and open Administration → ChatGPT connections. User roles are under People and permissions. New users normally receive Shared read access; editing and protected spaces need additional permission.

Keep full authorization URLs, passwords and tokens private.

2 · Add your workflow

Download your C0 memory skill

This skill tells ChatGPT which C0 instance to use and how to search, cite and save knowledge safely.

Download .skill

Open ChatGPT Skills, choose New skill → Upload, and select the downloaded file. Start a new chat after installation.

Skill upload depends on your ChatGPT account. The skill uses the connection from step 1; it does not sign you in or give extra permissions.

Or download the C0 plugin

The plugin includes setup, research and knowledge-maintenance workflows for this instance.

Open the C0 connection you created in ChatGPT and copy its page address. You can also paste its technical ID, starting with asdk_app_, connector_ or templated_apps_. Check that it shows the Connection URL above.

In ChatGPT’s personal Plugins page, use the plugin archive upload where available. Choose this ZIP, then check that all three workflows and your C0 connection are included. Installation controls depend on your account or workspace.

Try it

Start a new ChatGPT conversation with C0 enabled:

Use my C0 memory to find what we know about [your topic]. Read the original evidence and cite your sources.

Research makes no changes. Saving knowledge needs Editor access and write consent.

Instructions for your assistant

Using knowledge safely

    Keep your selected space for every content operation. A denied request never permits switching to Shared. Cite original passages and preserve revision history.

    Document files are uploaded through an authorized REST integration or Source tools.

    Sessions and reconnecting

    C0 renews short access tokens within your session limits. Defaults are one hour idle and eight hours absolute; your administrator or sign-in provider may set a shorter bound. Reconnect after expiry or revocation. A repeated login prompt is a reason to check the account and connection.

    MCP text ingestion uses sources_ingest_text for selected plain text or Markdown up to 128 KiB of UTF-8. Binary document files use the authenticated REST upload or Source tools.
